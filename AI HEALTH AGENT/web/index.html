About SmartHealth

SmartHealth connects the website, Android app, Health Connect, and Galaxy Watch data into one patient monitoring workflow. The app gives wellness suggestions only and does not diagnose disease or replace professional medical care.

How The Watch Connection Works

1. Watch Records

Galaxy Watch 5 captures supported vitals such as heart rate during normal use.

2. Phone Syncs

Samsung Health syncs the watch data on the phone and shares approved data with Health Connect.

3. App Uploads

The Android app reads approved Health Connect data and saves it for patient review.

4. Staff Review

Doctor/staff users can view patient records while patients see non-diagnostic wellness suggestions.

Privacy & Health Data

Health data is sensitive. SmartHealth should only collect data the user approves, explain why it is needed, and allow users to manage Health Connect permissions on their phone. Suggestions shown by the app are screening prompts, not medical advice.
